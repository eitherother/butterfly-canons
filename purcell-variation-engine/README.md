# Variation Engine on Henry Purcell

_Variation Engine on Henry Purcell_ is an IDM and ambient engine for performing variations on a particular In Nomine by Henry Purcell. It can weave between ambient takes on the source recording through dance music that can be increasingly surreal where a kind of waltz rhythm haunts the beat.

Though the engine is recording-agnostic and released under the MIT License, I recorded a particular performance with [Phillip Serna](https://www.phillipwserna.com/)'s recording, released under CC Share Alike on [Wikimedia Commons](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Henry_Purcell_(1659-1695)_%E2%80%93_In_Nomine_%C3%A06,_Z.746_for_Treble,_Tenor_%26_Bass_Viols.ogg>). If you are intrigued by the recording below, I encourage you to support him on his [bandcamp site](https://bandcamp.com/phillipwserna). Note that the recording is released on CC Share Alike, the same license associated with the source recording in Wikimedia commons.
